Introduction 
This Operations Manual outlines the standardized processes and procedures for 
Maninos Capital, ensuring efficient operations, scalability, and compliance with Texas 
and U.S. federal regulations. The manual covers the entire value chain:  
1. Purchase of Mobile Homes. 
2. Customer Onboarding. 
3. Debt Raising for Acquisitions. 
4. Rent Collection and Portfolio Management. 
5. Home Delivery and Customer Loyalty.

institutionalize operations and scale its rent-to-own mobile home business in Texas. By 
standardizing processes, leveraging technology, and ensuring compliance, the 
company can achieve o perational efficiency, customer satisfaction, and sustainable

1. Purchase of Mobile Homes 
Objective:  
Efficiently acquire used mobile homes in excellent condition that meet predefined 
quality and investment criteria to ensure profitability and customer satisfaction. 
Procedures 
1. Market Research and Sourcing. 
- Identify target markets in Texas (e.g., Houston, Dallas, San Antonio) with high 
demand for affordable housing. 
- Source mobile homes through real estate platforms, auctions, direct sellers, and 
mobile home parks. 
- Use data analytics to assess market trends, pricing, and appreciation potential. 
2. Attribute Checklist for Purchase:. 
   -Physical Condition: 
- No structural damage (e.g., roof, foundation, walls). 
- Functional plumbing, electrical, and HVAC systems. 
- Cosmetic condition suitable for immediate rental or minor renovations (<$5,000). 
   - Location: 
- Proximity to employment hubs, schools, and amenities. 
- Located in mobile home parks with stable management and low lot rental fees.

many families, leading to a nationwide housing shortage and a surge in demand for alternative housing solutions.
The NAHB/Wells Fargo Index shows a dramatic decline: only 37% of families can afford a median-priced home in 2024, down 
from 78% in 2012.
